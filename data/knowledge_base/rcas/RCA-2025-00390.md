# INCIDENT INVESTIGATION REPORT: RCA-2025-00390
**Service:** Notification Service  
**Completion Date:** 2026-01-09T12:00:00Z  
**Linked Incidents:** INC-2025-01581  
**Investigation Owner:** Messaging Platform  
**Root Cause Category:** Configuration Error  
**Business Impact:** EUR 23,900 (estimated)  

## Summary
Notification delivery stopped during the Christmas campaign because the RabbitMQ broker blocked all publishers after its disk filled up.

## Timeline
- 17:40 UTC: RabbitMQ disk alarm on rabbitmq-prod-01.
- 18:10 UTC: Queue depth alert raised by Marketing Operations.
- 19:05 UTC: 30 GB of old broker logs removed.
- 19:30 UTC: Broker restarted and delivery resumed.

## Root Cause
The broker host had been rebuilt by hand without log rotation for /var/log/rabbitmq. Broker logs grew until the disk passed the RabbitMQ free space limit and the broker blocked publishers.

## Five Whys
1. Why did delivery stop? The broker blocked all publishers.
2. Why? The free disk space went under the 50 MB limit.
3. Why was the disk full? Broker logs were never rotated.
4. Why no rotation? The host was rebuilt by hand without the logrotate configuration.

## Contributing Factors
- **Configuration:** No logrotate for /var/log/rabbitmq.
- **Monitoring:** Disk warnings not alerted.
- **Process:** Manual host rebuild outside the image pipeline.

## Corrective Actions
Removed the old logs and added the RabbitMQ logrotate configuration to the broker host image (CHG-2026-00012).

## Preventive Actions
Alert at 85% disk usage on broker hosts.
