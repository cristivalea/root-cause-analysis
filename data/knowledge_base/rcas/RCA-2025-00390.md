# INCIDENT INVESTIGATION REPORT: RCA-2025-00390
**Service:** Notification Service  
**Completion Date:** 2025-12-20T21:00:00Z  
**Linked Incidents:** INC-2025-01581  
**Investigation Owner:** Messaging Reliability Team  

## Summary
Notification delivery slowed sharply during a high-volume campaign window because the broker disk filled and retries became blocked waiting for write availability.

## Timeline
- 18:10 UTC: Daily retention cleanup job did not rotate logs as expected.
- 18:52 UTC: RabbitMQ disk usage crossed 92% and delay alarms began firing.
- 19:15 UTC: Consumer workers entered a retry loop and queued messages accumulated.
- 20:05 UTC: Platform operations cleared the disk and restarted the broker workers.

## Root Cause
The log rotation policy failed to prune historical logs from the root partition, causing the RabbitMQ data directory to fill and block message writes under campaign load.

## Contributing Factors
Disk capacity monitoring did not include broker filesystem alarms and the cleanup job had a stale configuration after a host image refresh.

## Corrective Actions
Restored disk space, fixed log rotation policy, and added broker filesystem occupancy alerts tied to the platform monitoring layer.
