# INCIDENT INVESTIGATION REPORT: RCA-2024-00124
**Service:** Auth Service  
**Completion Date:** 2024-04-23T12:00:00Z  
**Linked Incidents:** INC-2024-00061, INC-2024-00415  
**Investigation Owner:** IAM Engineering  
**Root Cause Category:** Third-Party / Vendor  
**Business Impact:** EUR 18,500 (estimated)  

## Summary
The one-time password provider had a regional outage and our fallback channel had to be switched on by hand.

## Timeline
- 03:18 UTC: Incident raised.
- 03:22 UTC: Assigned to IAM Engineering.
- 05:09 UTC: Waiting for the vendor.
- 05:53 UTC: Reassigned to Network Engineering.
- 07:09 UTC: Workaround applied.
- 07:54 UTC: Service restored.

## Root Cause
The OTP provider's EU region was unavailable for 70 minutes. The automatic fallback to the e-mail channel was disabled after a false alarm the year before.

## Five Whys
1. Why could users not sign in? One-time codes were not delivered.
2. Why were they not delivered? The OTP provider returned 503 for the EU region.
3. Why did the fallback not start by itself? Automatic fallback was disabled.
4. Why was it disabled? A past false alarm triggered it wrongly and it was never re-enabled.

## Contributing Factors
- **Dependencies:** Single OTP provider region.
- **Configuration:** Automatic fallback switched off.
- **Monitoring:** No synthetic check for OTP delivery.

## Corrective Actions
Re-enabled the automatic fallback with a stricter trigger.

## Preventive Actions
Added a synthetic OTP delivery check every 5 minutes.
