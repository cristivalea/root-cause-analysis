# INCIDENT INVESTIGATION REPORT: RCA-2024-00148
**Service:** Payment API  
**Completion Date:** 2024-05-15T15:00:00Z  
**Linked Incidents:** INC-2024-00507, INC-2024-00511  
**Investigation Owner:** Payments Platform Engineering  
**Root Cause Category:** Third-Party / Vendor  
**Business Impact:** EUR 3,500 (estimated)  

## Summary
Card tokenisation calls failed for a share of checkouts while the vault provider ran a maintenance with reduced capacity.

## Timeline
- 12:48 UTC: Incident raised.
- 12:55 UTC: Assigned to Payments Platform Engineering.
- 13:23 UTC: Waiting for the vendor.
- 13:49 UTC: Workaround applied.
- 14:25 UTC: Service restored.

## Root Cause
The tokenisation vendor moved traffic to a reduced-capacity cluster during unannounced maintenance. Our client had no failover to the secondary region configured, so requests failed instead of being retried there.

## Five Whys
1. Why did checkouts fail? Card tokenisation calls returned HTTP 502.
2. Why did tokenisation return 502? The vendor's primary cluster was overloaded during its maintenance.
3. Why did the overload reach our customers? The client sent all traffic to the primary region only.
4. Why was there no regional failover? The failover setting was never enabled after the vendor onboarding.
5. Why was that not noticed? Vendor failover was not part of the onboarding checklist.

## Contributing Factors
- **Dependencies:** Single region dependency on the tokenisation vendor.
- **Process:** Vendor maintenance notices were not tracked by the Payments team.
- **Monitoring:** Alert fired on checkout failures only, not on vault latency.

## Corrective Actions
Enabled automatic failover to the secondary vault region.

## Preventive Actions
Added vendor failover to the onboarding checklist and subscribed the on-call team to vendor maintenance notices.
