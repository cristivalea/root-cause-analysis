# INCIDENT INVESTIGATION REPORT: RCA-2025-00305
**Service:** Auth Service  
**Completion Date:** 2025-10-24T10:00:00Z  
**Linked Incidents:** INC-2025-01305  
**Investigation Owner:** IAM Engineering  
**Root Cause Category:** Software Defect  
**Business Impact:** EUR 35,600 (estimated)  

## Summary
Auth Service pods were killed for memory during a traffic peak and part of the logins failed.

## Timeline
- 11:55 UTC: First OOMKilled pod.
- 12:10 UTC: Login failure alert.
- 12:40 UTC: Deployment scaled from 3 to 6 pods.
- 13:05 UTC: Login failure rate back to normal.

## Root Cause
The token introspection cache had no size limit. Under high load it grew until the JVM heap was full and the pods were killed.

## Five Whys
1. Why did logins fail? Auth Service pods were being killed and restarted.
2. Why were they killed? They used more memory than their limit.
3. Why so much memory? The token introspection cache grew without a limit.
4. Why no limit? The cache was added quickly as a performance fix without sizing.

## Contributing Factors
- **Capacity:** Heap sized for normal traffic only.
- **Change / Release:** Cache added without a size limit.
- **Monitoring:** No alert on heap usage.

## Corrective Actions
Limited the introspection cache to 50,000 entries.

## Preventive Actions
Load test before every campaign with twice the expected traffic.
