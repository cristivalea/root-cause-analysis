# INCIDENT INVESTIGATION REPORT: RCA-2024-00374
**Service:** Payroll Service  
**Completion Date:** 2024-12-08T16:00:00Z  
**Linked Incidents:** INC-2024-01398  
**Investigation Owner:** HR Systems Engineering  
**Root Cause Category:** Certificate / Credential Expiry  
**Business Impact:** EUR 3,500 (estimated)  

## Summary
The SSH key used to upload the salary file to the bank expired.

## Timeline
- 15:18 UTC: Incident raised.
- 15:27 UTC: Assigned to HR Systems Engineering.
- 15:57 UTC: Waiting for the vendor.
- 16:06 UTC: Reassigned to Network Engineering.
- 16:17 UTC: Workaround applied.
- 16:46 UTC: Service restored.

## Root Cause
The bank enforces yearly SSH key expiry. The key was renewed by hand every year and the renewal was forgotten after the owner left the team.

## Five Whys
1. Why was the file not delivered? The upload to the bank failed with an authentication error.
2. Why did authentication fail? The SSH key had expired.
3. Why was it not renewed? Renewal was a manual yearly task.
4. Why was it forgotten? The task belonged to one person who left the team.

## Contributing Factors
- **Process:** Manual yearly renewal with a single owner.
- **Monitoring:** No expiry alert for bank credentials.
- **Dependencies:** Bank enforces key expiry.

## Corrective Actions
Renewed the key and delivered the file.

## Preventive Actions
Bank credentials added to the certificate expiry monitoring with 30 days warning.
