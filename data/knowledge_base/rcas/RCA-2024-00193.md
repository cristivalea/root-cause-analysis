# INCIDENT INVESTIGATION REPORT: RCA-2024-00193
**Service:** Notification Service  
**Completion Date:** 2024-06-25T21:00:00Z  
**Linked Incidents:** INC-2024-00498, INC-2024-00681  
**Investigation Owner:** Messaging Platform  
**Root Cause Category:** Third-Party / Vendor  
**Business Impact:** EUR 20,000 (estimated)  

## Summary
The SMS provider rejected our connections after rotating its credentials on its side.

## Timeline
- 14:47 UTC: Incident raised.
- 14:55 UTC: Assigned to Messaging Platform.
- 15:21 UTC: Waiting for the vendor.
- 15:38 UTC: Workaround applied.
- 15:58 UTC: Service restored.

## Root Cause
The SMS provider enforced new bind credentials after a security incident on its side. The notice reached a shared mailbox that nobody watched, so our workers kept using the old password.

## Five Whys
1. Why were SMS not delivered? The provider rejected the connection (bind failed).
2. Why was the bind rejected? The provider required new credentials.
3. Why did we not have them? The notice went to an unmonitored mailbox.
4. Why unmonitored? Vendor contacts were never updated after the team reorganisation.

## Contributing Factors
- **Dependencies:** Single SMS provider without automatic failover.
- **Process:** Vendor notices sent to an unowned mailbox.
- **Monitoring:** Bind failures logged but not alerted.

## Corrective Actions
Updated the credentials and enabled automatic failover to the secondary provider.

## Preventive Actions
Vendor contact list reviewed every quarter; bind failures now page the on-call engineer.
