# INCIDENT INVESTIGATION REPORT: RCA-2025-00192
**Service:** Settlement Service  
**Completion Date:** 2025-07-25T09:30:00Z  
**Linked Incidents:** INC-2025-00982  
**Investigation Owner:** Treasury Engineering  
**Root Cause Category:** Configuration Error  
**Business Impact:** EUR 12,800 (estimated)  

## Summary
Settlement event processing paused repeatedly because the consumer group kept rebalancing during the end-of-day peak.

## Timeline
- 22:05 UTC: First consumer left the group after a poll timeout.
- 22:18 UTC: Settlement backlog alert.
- 23:10 UTC: max.poll.interval.ms raised and the group restarted.
- 23:30 UTC: Backlog cleared.

## Root Cause
Slow ledger writes during the end-of-day peak made each batch take longer than max.poll.interval.ms. Consumers were removed from the group, which triggered a rebalance storm and stopped processing.

## Five Whys
1. Why did processing stop? The consumer group was rebalancing again and again.
2. Why? Consumers were removed after exceeding max.poll.interval.ms.
3. Why did they exceed it? Each batch took longer because ledger writes were slow at peak.
4. Why was the interval not enough? It was left at the default and never sized for peak load.

## Contributing Factors
- **Configuration:** Default max.poll.interval.ms.
- **Capacity:** Ledger database slow at the end-of-day peak.
- **Monitoring:** No alert on rebalance frequency.

## Corrective Actions
Raised max.poll.interval.ms to 600 seconds and reduced the batch size.

## Preventive Actions
Alert when the group rebalances more than 3 times in 10 minutes.
