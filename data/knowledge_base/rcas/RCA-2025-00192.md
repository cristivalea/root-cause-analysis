# INCIDENT INVESTIGATION REPORT: RCA-2025-00192
**Service:** Settlement Service  
**Completion Date:** 2025-08-12T16:40:00Z  
**Linked Incidents:** INC-2025-00982  
**Investigation Owner:** Reconciliation Engineering  

## Summary
Settlement event processing stalled for several minutes during a peak cash movement window, causing delayed clearing and blocked downstream ledger updates.

## Timeline
- 11:10 UTC: Kafka consumer group resumed after a previous maintenance restart.
- 11:33 UTC: Deadlocked consumer thread blocked deserialization of a malformed settlement payload.
- 11:41 UTC: Event partitions stopped advancing and queue lag rose sharply.
- 12:05 UTC: Engineering restarted the consumer group to clear the poisoned partition backlog.

## Root Cause
A malformed settlement payload triggered a poison-pill deserialization path in the Kafka consumer group, blocking the partition and deadlocking the event loop for downstream settlement workers.

## Contributing Factors
Insufficient payload validation in the event ingest layer and missing dead-letter handling for malformed settlement messages.

## Corrective Actions
Added schema validation before deserialization, enabled a dead-letter topic for invalid payloads, and expanded monitoring for consumer lag and partition stalls.
