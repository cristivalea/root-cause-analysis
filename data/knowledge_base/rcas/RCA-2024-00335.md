# INCIDENT INVESTIGATION REPORT: RCA-2024-00335
**Service:** Billing Service  
**Completion Date:** 2024-11-02T04:00:00Z  
**Linked Incidents:** INC-2024-00848, INC-2024-01267  
**Investigation Owner:** Finance Engineering  
**Root Cause Category:** Capacity / Resource Exhaustion  
**Business Impact:** EUR 12,500 (estimated)  

## Summary
PDF rendering for invoices with thousands of lines ran out of memory and timed out.

## Timeline
- 00:26 UTC: Incident raised.
- 00:36 UTC: Assigned to Finance Engineering.
- 01:43 UTC: Workaround applied.
- 02:01 UTC: Service restored.

## Root Cause
The renderer loaded all invoice lines into one HTML page. For accounts above 1,500 lines the headless browser ran out of memory and was restarted, so the job hit its timeout.

## Five Whys
1. Why were invoices missing? PDF generation timed out.
2. Why did it time out? The renderer process restarted while rendering.
3. Why did it restart? It ran out of memory on very large invoices.
4. Why so much memory? All lines were rendered into a single page.

## Contributing Factors
- **Capacity:** Renderer memory limit sized for small invoices.
- **Change / Release:** Large enterprise customers onboarded without a load test.
- **Monitoring:** No alert on renderer restarts.

## Corrective Actions
Split large invoices into pages of 500 lines.

## Preventive Actions
Added renderer restart alerting and a load test with the largest account.
