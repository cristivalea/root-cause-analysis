# INCIDENT INVESTIGATION REPORT: RCA-2024-00036
**Service:** Payment API  
**Completion Date:** 2024-02-02T15:00:00Z  
**Linked Incidents:** INC-2024-00034, INC-2024-00074  
**Investigation Owner:** Payments Platform Engineering  
**Root Cause Category:** Third-Party / Vendor  
**Business Impact:** EUR 15,500 (estimated)  

## Summary
Card authorisations timed out at peak hours because the acquirer throttled connections above the contracted rate.

## Timeline
- 08:31 UTC: Incident raised.
- 08:33 UTC: Assigned to Payments Platform Engineering.
- 08:48 UTC: Waiting for the vendor.
- 09:03 UTC: Workaround applied.
- 09:15 UTC: Service restored.

## Root Cause
Peak traffic exceeded the concurrent connection limit agreed with the acquirer. The acquirer queued the extra connections and our 10 second timeout expired.

## Five Whys
1. Why did card payments fail? Authorisation calls to the acquirer timed out.
2. Why did they time out? The acquirer queued connections above the contracted limit.
3. Why were we above the limit? Peak traffic grew 40% since the contract was sized.
4. Why was the limit not raised? Capacity reviews did not include vendor contract limits.

## Contributing Factors
- **Capacity:** Contracted connection limit too low for current peak.
- **Dependencies:** Single acquirer for card payments.
- **Process:** Vendor limits missing from capacity planning.

## Corrective Actions
Raised the contracted limit and enabled the secondary acquirer for overflow traffic.

## Preventive Actions
Quarterly capacity review now includes every vendor contract limit.
