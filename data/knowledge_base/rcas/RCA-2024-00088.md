# INCIDENT INVESTIGATION REPORT: RCA-2024-00088
**Service:** API Gateway  
**Completion Date:** 2024-10-02T10:00:00Z  
**Linked Incidents:** INC-2024-00891  
**Investigation Owner:** Core Platform  
**Root Cause Category:** Certificate / Credential Expiry  
**Business Impact:** EUR 62,000 (estimated)  

## Summary
The wildcard TLS certificate of the edge proxy nodes expired during the night and all clients failed the TLS handshake with the public API.

## Timeline
- 03:00 UTC: Wildcard certificate expired.
- 03:10 UTC: Partners and mobile apps report connection failures.
- 03:55 UTC: Expired certificate identified on the edge nodes.
- 04:40 UTC: Renewed certificate installed by hand.

## Root Cause
The wildcard certificate was renewed by hand once a year. The reminder was sent to a former team member and the renewal was missed.

## Five Whys
1. Why did clients fail to connect? The TLS handshake failed on every edge node.
2. Why did it fail? The wildcard certificate had expired.
3. Why was it not renewed? Renewal was a manual yearly task.
4. Why was the task missed? The reminder went to a person who had left the team.

## Contributing Factors
- **Process:** Manual yearly renewal with one owner.
- **Monitoring:** No alert on certificate expiry.
- **Configuration:** One wildcard certificate for all edge nodes.

## Corrective Actions
Installed a renewed certificate and moved the edge certificates to automatic renewal with cert-manager (CHG-2024-00731).

## Preventive Actions
Alert 30 days before any certificate expiry.
